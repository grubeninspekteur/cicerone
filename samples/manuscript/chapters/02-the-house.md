# The House on Tallow Row

## Tuesday evening

The house smelled of paraffin and of the sea getting in. Ines found the kitchen table laid for two, the plates filmed with dust, and under the second plate an envelope with no name on it.

Inside was a page cut from a ledger: columns of dates and weights in her mother's hand, and beside each one a single initial. T. T. T. Then, from January on, nothing but A. [[decide what the weights are before chapter 5]]

## Wednesday

Tobias Wren came to the door before she had finished her tea. He was nineteen and looked as if he had been told to be somewhere else.

"Mrs Aldous says you'll want the boat," he said.

"I haven't asked for a boat."

"No," said Tobias. "She says you will, though."
