# The Skerry

## Thursday, before light

They went out on the ebb, Tobias rowing and Ines in the stern with the ledger page folded inside her glove. Nobody had been to the Skerry since March. Tobias said so twice.

The landing was a slab of rock with an iron ring let into it. Above the tide line stood a salt pan, long disused, and in the pan a tin box wrapped in oilcloth. [[is a salt pan plausible this far north?]]

Ines took the pencil from the box in her right hand and wrote the date at the foot of the last column, because it seemed the thing her mother would have done. Then she read the column from the top.

The weights were not weights. They were tides.
